First Year (FE)

Mathematics-I: Engineering mathematics fundamentals.

Physics: Basic concepts of mechanics and waves.

Basic Electrical: Introduction to circuits.

Engineering Graphics: Technical drawing skills.

Second Year (SE)

Data Structures: Arrays, stacks, queues, trees.

OOP: Object-oriented programming concepts.

Digital Logic: Logic gates and circuits.

Discrete Mathematics: Logic and combinatorics.

Third Year (TE)

DBMS: Database design and SQL.

Operating System: Process and memory management.

Computer Networks: Networking concepts.

Software Engineering: Development lifecycle.

Final Year (BE)

Machine Learning: AI and predictive models.

Cloud Computing: Distributed systems.

Cyber Security: Network and data protection.

Project Work: Major final year project.
